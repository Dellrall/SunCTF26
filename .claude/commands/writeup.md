Generate or refine a complete CTF writeup for the challenge in the current directory.

### Structure
Populate `writeup.md` with:
1. **Challenge Overview**: Name, category, points, initial prompt.
2. **Analysis**: Core vulnerability, cryptographic flaw, or reversing findings.
3. **Exploit Walkthrough**: Step-by-step mechanism of the attack.
4. **Reproducible Script**: Working `solve.py` snippet.
5. **Captured Flag**: Final flag string.
6. **Key Takeaways & Mitigation**: Educational summary and defense recommendations.
