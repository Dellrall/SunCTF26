biubiubiu — macOS Apple Silicon build

Requirements:
- macOS on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/M3/M4 or newer)

Controls:
- W/A/S/D or arrow keys: move
- SPACE: fire
- E: prepare replay
- ENTER: commit replay
- F: verify cartridge state
- R: restart after game over

After reaching the victory screen, take a full screenshot, open a ticket in
the event Discord, and submit it to challenge author @salad for manual
verification and flag issuance.
